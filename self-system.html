The .Self system

One house. Three rooms.

Finding the Self, Witness.Self, and Reflect.Self are three different shapes — a blog, a meditation practice, a place to write — pointing at the same thing: the part of you that's aware, watching all of it happen.

The ethos

Watching is where it starts

Most meditation apps ask you to relax. This asks you to notice — the difference between calming the mind down and stepping back far enough to see it happening at all.

That move has an old name. Kashmir Shaivism — the tradition much of this site draws from — calls it pratyabhijñā: recognition. Not learning something new. Recognizing what was already true — that there's an awareness watching the thinking, and it was never actually disturbed by what passed through it.

But recognition is the door, not the room. Trika doesn't stop at "just watch" — it points further, toward spanda, the pulse underneath the watching, and vimarśa, awareness's own capacity to know itself. Watching is honest. It's also just where this starts.
