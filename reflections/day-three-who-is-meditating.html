Day Three — Who Is Meditating?

Sitting in meditation today, concentrating on the Agni Chakra, I noticed the body felt like it wasn't there at all. Not cluttered — empty. And in that emptiness, whenever a thought arose, I kept returning to the same question: who am I? The mind always needs something to latch onto, and thoughts don't stop just because we ask them to. But we can choose where the mind goes — toward that one question, again and again.
