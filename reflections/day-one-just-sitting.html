Day One — Just Sitting

First real sit in a while. Mostly noticing how loud the mind is in the first five minutes — the to-do list, a half-finished conversation, a song stuck on repeat. By the end there were a few seconds where none of that was running. Not dramatic. Just quieter. Starting here, and starting honest: most of this practice is just noticing I've wandered off, and coming back.
