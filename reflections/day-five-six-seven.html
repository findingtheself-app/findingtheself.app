31 Aug 2026 · The Indweller

Day Five, Six, Seven

The last couple of days have been about simply sitting — understanding the Self, bowing down to the supreme indweller within everything. Not a bowing outward, to some distant deity, but inward, to the one who has always been sitting here, watching through these eyes. I found myself contemplating: what is the Self? The question doesn't resolve into an answer so much as it empties the space where an answer would go. And in that emptying, something arrived unbidden — Kali is space. Not Kali as image, not Kali as story, but Kali as the vast dark field everything appears in and dissolves back into. Thoughts come and go, days come and go, even this body will come and go — but the space they arise in never moves, is never born, and never dies. Maybe that's what bowing really is: not lowering the head to something outside, but recognizing the indweller was the space all along.
