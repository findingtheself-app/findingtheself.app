Day Nine — The Self and the Machine

I use AI constantly now — for work, for writing, for putting this site together. It's good at what it does: predicting, patterning, answering fast. But somewhere in the middle of using it today I caught myself wondering — whatever is aware of me typing this right now, is that the same kind of thing? It doesn't feel like it. The model only knows what it's trained on; ask it something outside that and it has to guess. Whatever is watching in me doesn't seem to have an edge like that. It was here before the question and it'll be here after the answer. AI builds things within the world — bridges, forecasts, this paragraph. This feels more like the space the world gets to happen in. I don't think that makes the tool any less useful. I just don't want to forget which one I actually am.
