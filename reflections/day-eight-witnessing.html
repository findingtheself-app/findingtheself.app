Day Eight — Witnessing

When you think about it, there is little we do as life and the universe unfolds in front of us — all predetermined, all in a way. Do we really watch and see what is with an empty mind? This is very hard; I try to just see it as it is. This is a practice I am trying, but it's very hard.
