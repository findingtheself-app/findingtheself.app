Day Four — The Indweller, With I Bow to Thee

I sat in silence and felt light — saw the light — when I closed my eyes. A feeling came that I am not the body, not the ego, not the mind, but the Self, who bows to the Self within all. The problems and anxieties we think we have aren't real; the Self only watches, while the Leela of the universe plays out.
